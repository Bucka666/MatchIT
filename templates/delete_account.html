Delete Your GrailSweep Account & Data

Last updated: May 2026

GrailSweep (grailsweep.com) respects your right to delete your account and data at any time. This page explains how.

Option 1: Delete Specific Data Only (Keep Account)

You can delete specific data without closing your account:

  • Individual cards in your collection: Open GrailSweep → Collection page → tap the ✕ Remove button on any card tile.
  • All cards in your collection: Open GrailSweep → Collection page → tap the 🗑 Clear All button in the toolbar. You will be asked to confirm before deletion.
  • Scan history: Email support@grailsweep.com with your code and request that your scan counter be reset.
  • Price alerts: Email support@grailsweep.com with your code and the card name(s) you want to cancel alerts for. We will remove them from your subscription within 30 days (usually within 1 business day).
  • Device fingerprints: Email support@grailsweep.com with your code to request a device list reset. Useful if you have changed phones.

Option 2: Delete Your Entire Account & All Associated Data

Email support@grailsweep.com from the email address tied to your subscription with the subject “Delete my account”. We will complete the deletion within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you no longer have access to your original email, contact support@grailsweep.com with your GRAIL-XXXX-XXXX code and we will verify ownership before processing.

What Gets Deleted

When you request full account deletion, we remove:

  • Your subscription record (email, GRAIL-XXXX-XXXX code, tier, status)
  • All collection data tied to your code
  • Scan history and saved match results
  • Device fingerprints associated with your code
  • Referral records and any pending referral credits
  • Price alert subscriptions
  • Any feedback you submitted that is linked to your code

What Is Retained

For legal and operational reasons, the following data is retained after account deletion:

  • Stripe transaction records: Retained for 6 years to comply with UK HMRC tax record-keeping requirements. These records do not contain your GrailSweep code after deletion — only the transaction ID and amount.
  • Aggregate anonymous analytics: Total scan counts and similar metrics are retained but contain no personal identifiers or links to you.
  • Server logs: Rotated automatically after 30 days. May briefly contain IP addresses and request data but are not used for any analysis after rotation.

Timeframe

  • Account deletion requests are completed within 30 days
  • Partial data deletion (Option 1 in-app actions) is immediate
  • Email confirmation is sent once full deletion is complete

Contact

If you have any questions about deletion or your data, email support@grailsweep.com.
