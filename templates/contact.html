Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate is the card identification?
GrailSweep uses advanced AI visual matching combined with OCR text recognition. Accuracy varies by card condition, lighting and photo quality. Modern cards in good condition typically achieve 85-95% accuracy.
Which TCGs are supported?
We currently support Pokémon TCG, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh! and One Piece TCG with a database of 165,000+ cards.
I paid but haven't received my access code.
Check your spam folder first. If you still haven't received it after 30 minutes, email us at support@grailsweep.com with your payment confirmation and we'll issue your code manually.
Can I use GrailSweep on multiple devices?
Your access code can be entered on any device. Your collection data is stored locally on each device separately.
Can I use GrailSweep on multiple devices?
Your Pro access follows your Apple ID — sign in with the same Apple ID on any device to access Pro. Your collection data is stored locally on each device separately.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Use the 'Manage My Subscription' section below to access the Stripe customer portal where you can cancel anytime. You'll retain access until the end of your billing period. If you have any trouble, email us at support@grailsweep.com and we'll help.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions on your iPhone or iPad to cancel anytime. You keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. If you have any trouble, email us at support@grailsweep.com and we'll help.
Are prices accurate?
Prices are sourced from TCGPlayer and Cardmarket and are updated regularly. They are for reference only and may not reflect current market conditions. Always verify prices before buying or selling.
