Features

Everything a collector needs

From instant identification to price tracking and marketplace links — built for serious collectors who want speed and accuracy across all major TCGs.

Live Camera Scanner
Point your phone at any card. Auto-detects, identifies, and prices in seconds. Works with scanner stands for bulk cataloguing.
Advanced Visual AI
Proprietary multi-model AI that matches artwork, not text. Handles worn cards, different printings, and real-world photo conditions.
Instant GBP Pricing
Market prices from TCGPlayer, Cardmarket, and more — automatically converted to pounds. Know what any card is worth instantly.
7 Marketplace Links

Batch Scanning Sessions
Scan your entire collection in one session. Running total, best card alert, CSV export, duplicate detection, and rarity breakdown.
Developer API
Integrate card identification into your own app, website, or till system. Send a photo, get back the match — simple to plug into any platform.
