Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 2026

1. Who We Are
GrailSweep is an AI-powered trading card identification and price reference tool, operated from the United Kingdom. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about how we handle your data. This policy is governed by UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018.

2. What Data We Collect
Images you upload: Card photos you submit for identification are processed by our AI matching system. Cropped regions containing card text (set codes, card numbers) are also processed by Google Cloud Vision for optical character recognition. Images are temporarily retained for processing and brief operational purposes (e.g. error diagnostics, image quality verification) and are routinely deleted thereafter.

Camera access: When you use our live scanner feature, GrailSweep accesses your device's camera to capture card images for identification. The camera stream is processed within your browser; only individual frames you choose to scan are transmitted to our servers, and these are handled the same as uploaded images. We do not record video.

Usage data: We collect anonymous usage statistics (scan counts, match accuracy feedback, scan timing) to improve the service. This data is not linked to identifiable individuals.

Payment data: If you subscribe to GrailSweep Pro, payments are processed by Stripe. We do not store your card details — Stripe handles all payment information securely under PCI DSS compliance.

Collection data: Your card collection is stored locally on your device using browser localStorage. A copy is also synchronised to our servers (via your access code) so it can be restored across devices and browser refreshes. We do not link this data to other personal identifiers.

Email address: If you purchase a Pro subscription, we collect your email address to send your access code and transactional updates.

Access codes: Pro subscriptions are validated via unique access codes (e.g. GRAIL-XXXX-XXXX). These are stored on our servers to verify subscription status.

3. How We Use Your Data
- To identify trading cards and return match results
- To process payments and deliver access codes
- To improve the accuracy of our AI matching engine
- To send transactional emails (access codes, subscription updates)
- To synchronise your collection across devices
- To diagnose technical issues and improve service reliability

4. Data Sharing
We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the following processors, who act on our behalf under data-processing agreements:

- Stripe — for payment processing
- Google Cloud Vision — for optical character recognition on cropped card-image regions (set codes and card numbers)
- Modal — our cloud infrastructure provider, where image processing and AI matching occur
- Cloudflare — for content delivery, security, and DDoS protection (Cloudflare sees inbound requests but does not access processed data)

5. Data Retention
- Card images: retained briefly during processing and for short-term operational purposes, then deleted
- Email addresses: retained for as long as your subscription is active, plus 30 days after cancellation for support purposes
- Collection data: retained while your access code remains active; deleted on request
- Usage statistics: retained in aggregated, anonymised form indefinitely
- Payment records: retained by Stripe per their policies; we do not retain card details ourselves

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us.

6. International Data Transfers
Some of our processors (notably Google Cloud Vision and Stripe) may process data outside the United Kingdom and European Economic Area. Where this occurs, we rely on Standard Contractual Clauses or equivalent safeguards approved under UK GDPR.

7. Children
GrailSweep is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us at grailsweep@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

8. Your Rights
Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Request correction of inaccurate data
- Request deletion of your data ("right to erasure")
- Restrict or object to processing
- Data portability (receive your data in a transferable format)
- Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact us at grailsweep@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk if you believe we have not handled your data appropriately.

9. Cookies and Local Storage
GrailSweep uses browser localStorage and a small number of essential cookies (such as gs_access_code for subscription identification). We do not use tracking cookies, analytics cookies, or advertising cookies.

10. Security
We use industry-standard security measures including:
- HTTPS encryption for all data in transit
- Cloudflare DDoS protection and bot mitigation
- Secure storage of access codes on our servers
- Stripe PCI DSS compliance for payment handling
- Restricted access controls on our internal systems

While we take security seriously, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. We commit to notifying affected users within 72 hours if a personal data breach occurs that poses a risk to their rights.
